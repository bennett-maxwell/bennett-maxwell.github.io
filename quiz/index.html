How the Fit Score works

Capital fit compares your numbers with the brand's Item 7 total investment range in its FDD. Full marks when your maximum investment covers the top of the brand's range and your liquid capital covers its minimum; the score scales down as your numbers fall short, and it is 0 when your maximum is below the brand's minimum. Where a brand discloses a liquid capital or net worth requirement, that disclosed figure is checked directly and the check is named on the result card.

Location is state-level: a brand with outlets in your target state scores on proven presence, and an exclusive- or protected-territory brand with no outlets in your target state scores highest and carries the catalog's one availability label. Risk reads only disclosed signals — closures, litigation, bankruptcy, unit trend. Timeline compares your desired opening timeline with the brand's typical time to open, where its FDD discloses one.

How to read this catalog. Every figure comes from the brand's own Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD). The fee is Item 5. The investment range is Item 7. Royalty is Item 6. The territory rule is Item 12. Outlet counts by state are Item 20. Territory availability is shown the way the FDD supports it. We state what a brand's territory rule says. We also state where its existing units are. We never claim a specific area is available. Only the franchisor can confirm that. Data assembled 2026-10-26; verify current terms with the franchisor before investing.
