How much does a Health & Fitness franchise cost?

Across the 550 Health & Fitness brands whose FDD states a total investment range, the median total initial investment is $147,750 – $324,638 (Item 7). The median midpoint is $243,950, the middle 50% of brands fall between midpoints of $100,344 and $475,844, and the median franchise fee is $40,000 (middle 50%: $25,000–$54,900, n = 444).

Coverage and method for this page

This category page covers 550 of the 612 Health & Fitness brands in the Franchise KI catalog; brands whose extraction does not state both ends of the Item 7 range are excluded, never estimated. Method in brief: each figure is the total initial investment range disclosed in Item 7 of that brand's FDD of record, as extracted into the Franchise KI catalog. A field an FDD does not state stays empty — no estimates are used. Full method: Methodology. Every brand named above is a row in dataset.csv.
