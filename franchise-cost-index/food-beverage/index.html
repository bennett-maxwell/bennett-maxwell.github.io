How much does a Food & Beverage franchise cost?

Across the 998 Food & Beverage brands whose FDD states a total investment range, the median total initial investment is $275,250 – $616,750 (Item 7). The median midpoint is $455,759, the middle 50% of brands fall between midpoints of $225,056 and $862,375, and the median franchise fee is $35,000 (middle 50%: $25,000–$40,000, n = 773).

Coverage and method for this page

This category page covers 998 of the 1130 Food & Beverage brands in the Franchise KI catalog; brands whose extraction does not state both ends of the Item 7 range are excluded, never estimated. Method in brief: each figure is the total initial investment range disclosed in Item 7 of that brand's FDD of record, as extracted into the Franchise KI catalog. A field an FDD does not state stays empty — no estimates are used. Full method: Methodology. Every brand named above is a row in dataset.csv.
