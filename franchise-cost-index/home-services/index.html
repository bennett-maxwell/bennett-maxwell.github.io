How much does a Home Services franchise cost?

Across the 199 Home Services brands whose FDD states a total investment range, the median total initial investment is $112,500 – $204,478 (Item 7). The median midpoint is $157,250, the middle 50% of brands fall between midpoints of $121,130 and $235,050, and the median franchise fee is $49,500 (middle 50%: $35,000–$59,500, n = 186).

Coverage and method for this page

This category page covers 199 of the 237 Home Services brands in the Franchise KI catalog; brands whose extraction does not state both ends of the Item 7 range are excluded, never estimated. Method in brief: each figure is the total initial investment range disclosed in Item 7 of that brand's FDD of record, as extracted into the Franchise KI catalog. A field an FDD does not state stays empty — no estimates are used. Full method: Methodology. Every brand named above is a row in dataset.csv.
