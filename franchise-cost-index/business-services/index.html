How much does a Business Services franchise cost?

Across the 257 Business Services brands whose FDD states a total investment range, the median total initial investment is $131,000 – $252,450 (Item 7). The median midpoint is $198,275, the middle 50% of brands fall between midpoints of $90,200 and $447,294, and the median franchise fee is $35,000 (middle 50%: $17,625–$50,000, n = 251).

Coverage and method for this page

This category page covers 257 of the 263 Business Services brands in the Franchise KI catalog; brands whose extraction does not state both ends of the Item 7 range are excluded, never estimated. Method in brief: each figure is the total initial investment range disclosed in Item 7 of that brand's FDD of record, as extracted into the Franchise KI catalog. A field an FDD does not state stays empty — no estimates are used. Full method: Methodology. Every brand named above is a row in dataset.csv.
