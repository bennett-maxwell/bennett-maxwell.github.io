Franchise territories, honestly mapped

Ask most sites where you can open a franchise and they'll show you a guess. A territory question has a real answer, and it lives in each brand's FDD:

The three places the answer lives

1 · Item 12 — the rule. Every FDD states what territory you get: an exclusive territory (the franchisor cannot place another unit inside it), a protected territory (partial protection), non-exclusive (you get no exclusivity at all), or no territory. We have extracted and verified the Item 12 classification for 2,079 brands.

2 · Item 20 — the map. The FDD's outlet tables disclose where the brand's franchised units actually are, state by state. For 400 brands we publish that map on the brand's page.

3 · The exclusion logic. Put the rule and the map together and you can see the negative space: an area inside an existing franchisee's exclusive or protected territory is not available while that agreement runs. A state where the FDD discloses no units and no projected openings carries the label "Most likely available" — no existing or announced outlets here, based on the brand's latest FDD (Item 20). Only the franchisor can confirm availability — the label is an inference from the FDD, never a promise.

Worked example

Take any large brand in the catalog — open its page and read its territory section: the Item 12 words first, then the state map of its existing units, then the list of states with no disclosed presence. That is how you research a brand like McDonald's or a regional concept with the same rigor: see where they are, read the rule that governs where they can go next, and treat the empty states as candidates to verify with the franchisor — not as guarantees.

Exclusive territory — 465 brands

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, the franchisor grants an exclusive territory: while your agreement runs, the franchisor may not place another franchisee — or operate a competing company unit — inside your territory's boundaries (subject to the carve-outs in the FDD). Areas inside an existing franchisee's territory are therefore not available to a new buyer.
