PerfectFit Matching

Type in your information and every brand in the catalog is graded 1–100 on how well it matches your criteria: Capital 25 · Ownership 20 · Risk 15 · Industry 15 · Location 15 · Timeline 10. Scores are computed on a 0–100 scale from FDD-stated data; brands that score 0 are not shown, so every score you see is between 1 and 100.

How to read a score: each card shows its six dimension sub-scores and a coverage line — “scored on N of 6”. A dimension a brand's FDD does not state is given a neutral default, is labelled “defaulted — not stated in the FDD”, and never counts toward that brand's coverage. FranchiseKI never estimates a missing value: the labeled 30%-of-investment liquid-capital estimate that exists for some brands is not used in any score. Location scores the territory terms stated in the FDD (Item 12) — it is not a geographic match, and nothing on this page claims that a territory is available anywhere; we only ever tell you when data shows a brand is not a fit. Your answers stay in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Where the data comes from

Brand fields are extracted from each brand's Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD): investment range (Item 7), territory terms (Item 12), timeline and operations fields, plus the pilot scoring set (ownership styles, derived risk tier, industry tags) for 498 brands. This page merges the catalog's own match index (4,131 brands) with that pilot scoring overlay at load time, in your browser. A field an FDD does not state stays empty — it is labelled, never filled with an estimate.

FranchiseKI — FDD-sourced franchise data. PerfectFit scores are informational, computed from FDD-stated data as labelled on each card; only a franchisor can confirm territory availability, and this page never claims an area is available.
