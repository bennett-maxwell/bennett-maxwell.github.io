How to read the availability row

FranchiseKI never claims a specific area is available. For a brand whose Item 12 grants an exclusive or protected territory, the availability cell carries the catalog's one availability label, verbatim: “Most likely available — no existing or announced outlets here, based on the brand's latest FDD (Item 20). Only the franchisor can confirm availability.” — followed by the states where that brand's latest FDD discloses no existing units and no projected openings. Areas inside an existing franchisee's exclusive or protected territory are not available while that agreement runs. A brand with no exclusivity has no territory-based exclusions at all. Only the franchisor can confirm availability.

Where a brand's FDD does not disclose a figure, the cell reads “Not disclosed in the FDD” — we would rather show the gap than fill it with an estimate. That is the common case in franchise disclosure, not an error: many FDDs leave a fee, a royalty, or the Item 12 text itself to be confirmed with the franchisor.

How to read this catalog. Every figure comes from the brand's own Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) — franchise fees and investment ranges from Items 5–7, royalty from Item 6, territory rules from Item 12, and outlet counts by state from Item 20. Territory availability is shown the way the FDD supports it: we state what a brand's territory rule says and where its existing units are. We never claim a specific area is available — only the franchisor can confirm that. Data assembled 2026-10-26; verify current terms with the franchisor before investing.
