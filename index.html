How territory availability works here

Most franchise sites guess. We don't. For every brand with an extracted Item 12 profile, its page shows: (1) the territory rule in the FDD's own words, (2) where the brand's existing units are, state by state, and (3) what that means — areas inside an existing franchisee's exclusive or protected territory are not available, and states with no disclosed units are where availability is most plausible (an inference from the FDD, labeled as such — never a promise). How to read this catalog. Every figure comes from the brand's own Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) — franchise fees and investment ranges from Items 5–7, royalty from Item 6, territory rules from Item 12, and outlet counts by state from Item 20. Territory availability is shown the way the FDD supports it: we state what a brand's territory rule says and where its existing units are. We never claim a specific area is available — only the franchisor can confirm that. Data assembled 2026-10-26; verify current terms with the franchisor before investing.
