How territory availability works here

Most franchise sites guess. We don't. For every brand with an extracted Item 12 profile, its page shows: (1) the territory rule in the FDD's own words, (2) where the brand's existing units are, state by state, and (3) what that means — areas inside an existing franchisee's exclusive or protected territory are not available, and states with no existing or announced outlets carry the label "Most likely available" — no existing or announced outlets there, based on the brand's latest FDD (Item 20). Only the franchisor can confirm availability. Read the full territory guide →

How to read this catalog. Every figure comes from the brand's own Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD). The fee is Item 5. The investment range is Item 7. Royalty is Item 6. The territory rule is Item 12. Outlet counts by state are Item 20. Territory availability is shown the way the FDD supports it. We state what a brand's territory rule says. We also state where its existing units are. We never claim a specific area is available. Only the franchisor can confirm that. Data assembled 2026-10-26; verify current terms with the franchisor before investing.
