About Goldfish Swim School

Goldfish Swim School is a swim-school franchise for children ages 4 months to 12 years, offering group and private swim lessons, birthday parties, open swim times, and swim clinics, with a snack bar and retail area.

Protected territory — classified from Item 12, p.42 of the brand's FDD

Areas inside an existing franchisee's protected territory are generally not available to a new buyer for a directly competing unit; the exact limits are in the FDD text quoted above.
