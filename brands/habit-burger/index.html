About The Habit Burger Grill

The Habit Burger Grill is a California-born fast-casual chain known for its char-grilled burgers, fresh ingredients, and made-to-order milkshakes since 1969. Backed by Yum! Brands, The Habit Burger offers franchisees strong unit economics and a growing national footprint. Founded in 1969 in Santa Barbara, California, the chain was acquired by Yum! Brands for $375 million in March 2020 and remains headquartered in Irvine, California.
