About Burn Boot Camp

Challenging group fitness franchise with a focus on community, empowerment, and total body transformation. FDD-SOURCED FIGURES (2025 FDD, Kline Franchising, Inc., issued Apr 30, 2025): total initial investment $281,899-$645,344 (Item 7, PDF p.24); initial franchise fee $60,000 (Item 5, PDF p.13); royalty 6% of Gross Revenues per Report Period (Item 6, PDF p.16); System Brand Fee 2% of Gross Revenues (Item 6, PDF p.16). OUTLETS (Item 20, Table 1, PDF p.77): 365 total outlets as of year-end 2024 (356 franchised + 9 company-owned). OWNERSHIP (Item 15, PDF p.60): the franchise must at all times be under the direct, day-to-day, full-time supervision of a franchisor-approved Operations Manager (the owner may serve as Operations Manager); a designated Lead Trainer oversees the training regimen.
