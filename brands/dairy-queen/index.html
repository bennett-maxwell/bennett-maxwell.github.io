About DAIRY QUEEN

DAIRY QUEEN is one of the world's most recognized quick-service restaurant franchises with 7,000+ locations across 20+ countries serving signature Blizzard treats, burgers, hot dogs, and more. Owned by Berkshire Hathaway, the brand offers franchisees a legacy system with proven unit economics, robust marketing support, and both traditional and DQ Grill & Chill formats.
