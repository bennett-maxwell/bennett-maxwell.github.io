About CRUMBL

Crumbl is a cookie bakery franchise founded in 2017 in Logan, Utah, by Jason McGowan and Sawyer Hemsley. The franchisor, Crumbl Franchising, LLC of Lindon, Utah, began franchising in February 2018 and operates a cookie baking and delivery system selling fresh, warm cookies plus ice cream and other complementary products. Per the 2026 FDD, the franchise fee is $50,000 [FDD: Crumbl Franchising, LLC, 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document, issued April 6, 2026 (amended May 13, 2026), Item 5], total initial investment ranges from $848,566 to $1,472,533 [FDD: Crumbl Franchising, LLC, 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document, issued April 6, 2026 (amended May 13, 2026), Item 7], royalties are 8% of gross sales plus a 2% marketing fund [FDD: Crumbl Franchising, LLC, 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document, issued April 6, 2026 (amended May 13, 2026), Item 6], and territories are non-exclusive. 1,101 franchised outlets were operating at year-end 2025 [FDD: Crumbl Franchising, LLC, 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document, issued April 6, 2026 (amended May 13, 2026), Item 20]. Item 19 reports average 2024 gross sales of $1,354,688 and average net profit of $251,706 across 858 reporting locations. The system grew from 327 outlets at the end of 2022 to 1,059 at the end of 2024, making Crumbl one of the fastest-scaling dessert franchises in the United States.
