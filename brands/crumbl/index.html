About CRUMBL

Crumbl is a cookie bakery franchise founded in 2017 in Logan, Utah, by Jason McGowan and Sawyer Hemsley. The franchisor, Crumbl Franchising, LLC of Lindon, Utah, began franchising in February 2018 and operates a cookie baking and delivery system selling fresh, warm cookies plus ice cream and other complementary products. Per the 2026 FDD, the franchise fee is $50,000 [FDD: Crumbl Franchising, LLC, 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document, issued April 6, 2026 (amended May 13, 2026), Item 5], total initial investment ranges from $848,566 to $1,472,533 [FDD: Crumbl Franchising, LLC, 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document, issued April 6, 2026 (amended May 13, 2026), Item 7], royalties are 8% of gross sales plus a 2% marketing fund [FDD: Crumbl Franchising, LLC, 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document, issued April 6, 2026 (amended May 13, 2026), Item 6], and territories are non-exclusive. 1,101 franchised outlets were operating at year-end 2025 [FDD: Crumbl Franchising, LLC, 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document, issued April 6, 2026 (amended May 13, 2026), Item 20]. Item 19 reports average 2024 gross sales of $1,354,688 and average net profit of $251,706 across 858 reporting locations. The system grew from 327 outlets at the end of 2022 to 1,059 at the end of 2024, making Crumbl one of the fastest-scaling dessert franchises in the United States. Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Non-exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.45 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, you do not receive an exclusive territory. The franchisor may sell additional franchises, operate company units, or sell through other channels in the same area. No area is reserved for you — and no area is closed to you by another franchisee's exclusivity either; placement is the franchisor's decision.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “territory is NOT exclusive - the FDD states you will not receive an exclusive territory.”

Availability

Because this brand grants no exclusive territory, there are no territory-based exclusions: no area is reserved for an existing franchisee, and no area is reserved for you. Whether a new unit is placed anywhere is the franchisor's decision under Item 12.
