About Dogtopia

Dogtopia is North America's leading and fastest-growing dog daycare, boarding, and spa franchise with over 200 locations. Founded in 2002 and headquartered in Phoenix, AZ, Dogtopia provides safe, fun, and educational environments where dogs can play, learn, and socialize.
