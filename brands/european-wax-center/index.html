About European Wax Center

MONEY FIGURES (European Wax Center 2026 Amended FDD, issued Apr 23, 2026, amended May 19, 2026): initial franchise fee $45,000 for new franchisees ($36,000 for existing franchisees) (cover, PDF p1); estimated initial investment $331,600-$776,950 (cover, PDF p1); Royalty Fee 6.00% of Gross Sales, paid weekly (Item 6, PDF p21); Marketing Fund 3.00% of Gross Sales, paid monthly (Item 6, PDF p21). OUTLETS (FDD Item 20 Table 1, PDF p75): 1,047 total outlets as of Jan 3, 2026. OWNERSHIP (FDD Item 15, PDF p62): each center must always be under the direct, full-time, daily supervision of a dedicated center manager; the franchisor may require an individual owner to serve as center manager (a trained designated manager is allowed). FDD AVAILABILITY: 2026 Amended FDD (issued Apr 23, 2026, amended May 19, 2026). TERRITORY (Item 12): territory is NOT exclusive - the FDD states you will not receive an exclusive territory.

Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Non-exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.55 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, you do not receive an exclusive territory. The franchisor may sell additional franchises, operate company units, or sell through other channels in the same area. No area is reserved for you — and no area is closed to you by another franchisee's exclusivity either; placement is the franchisor's decision.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “territory is NOT exclusive - the FDD states you will not receive an exclusive territory.”

Availability

Because this brand grants no exclusive territory, there are no territory-based exclusions: no area is reserved for an existing franchisee, and no area is reserved for you. Whether a new unit is placed anywhere is the franchisor's decision under Item 12.
