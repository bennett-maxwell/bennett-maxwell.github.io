About OYF US Inc

Our affiliate, OYF ITL Inc., a corporation in Nova Scotia (Canada), began offering master franchises internationally but outside of Canada in September 2023. We began offering franchises for Oxygen Yoga & Fitness Facilities in August 2023. TERRITORY (Item 12): territory is NOT exclusive - the FDD states you will not receive an exclusive territory.

Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Non-exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.39 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, you do not receive an exclusive territory. The franchisor may sell additional franchises, operate company units, or sell through other channels in the same area. No area is reserved for you — and no area is closed to you by another franchisee's exclusivity either; placement is the franchisor's decision.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “territory is NOT exclusive - the FDD states you will not receive an exclusive territory.”

Availability

Because this brand grants no exclusive territory, there are no territory-based exclusions: no area is reserved for an existing franchisee, and no area is reserved for you. Whether a new unit is placed anywhere is the franchisor's decision under Item 12.
