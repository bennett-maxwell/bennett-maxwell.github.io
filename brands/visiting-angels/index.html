About Visiting Angels

VISITING ANGELS is a non-medical home care franchise founded in 1998 in Bryn Mawr, Pennsylvania. Franchisees provide in-home caregiving services to adult clients, employing caregivers who assist with bathing, dressing, personal care and companionship. Per the 2026 FDD (issued April 20, 2026): initial franchise fee $64,950 for a standard 200,000-population Protected Territory, ranging $51,950-$89,950 by population tier (Item 5, p.12); total initial investment $125,460-$171,150 (Item 7, p.25); service fee 3.5% of gross revenues plus 2.5% national marketing fee (Item 6, pp.13-14); 541 franchised outlets with 0 company-owned units as of 12/31/2025 (Item 20 Table No. 1, p.66); Protected Territory by zip-code boundaries, NOT exclusive (Item 12, pp.49-50); at least one 25%+ owner must manage full-time during the first 4 years, individual owners must directly supervise on premises (Item 15, p.59). [FDD: Living Assistance Services, Inc., 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document, issued April 20, 2026]
