About Baskin-Robbins

BASKIN-ROBBINS is the world's largest ice cream and frozen dessert franchise with 8,000+ locations in 50+ countries. The brand's iconic 31 Flavors concept and regular limited-time offerings keep consumers engaged, while franchisees benefit from a low initial investment, flexible store formats, and co-branding opportunities with sister brand Dunkin' for combined unit economics.

Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Non-exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.65 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, you do not receive an exclusive territory. The franchisor may sell additional franchises in the same area. It may also operate company units there. Or it may sell through other channels. No area is reserved for you. No area is closed to you by another franchisee's exclusivity either. Placement is the franchisor's decision.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “territory is NOT exclusive - the FDD states you will not receive an exclusive territory. We currently do business under our name, Baskin-Robbins Franchising LLC, and our brand name “Baskin-Robbins” and “BR.” The Baskin-Robbins brand began in 1946 and we, through our”

Availability

Because this brand grants no exclusive territory, there are no territory-based exclusions: no area is reserved for an existing franchisee, and no area is reserved for you. Whether a new unit is placed anywhere is the franchisor's decision under Item 12.
