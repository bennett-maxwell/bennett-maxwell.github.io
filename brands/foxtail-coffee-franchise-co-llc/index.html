About Foxtail Coffee Franchise Co., LLC

Foxtail Coffee is a thriving coffee franchise that was founded in 2016 in Winter Park, Florida, by Alex Tchekmeian and Iain Yeakle. The company started with a single location and quickly gained popularity for its commitment to quality coffee and a welcoming atmosphere. The company has opened in six states—Florida, Georgia, Michigan, Nevada, North Carolina and Virginia—and will soon debut in Alabama, Arizona, Maryland and South Carolina.
