About Embassy Suites Franchise LLC

Embassy Suites is an all-suite upper-upscale hotel brand franchised by HILTON FRANCHISE HOLDING LLC (2026 US FDD, issued March 30, 2026). The franchise application fee for new development or conversion is $100,000, plus $400 for each guest room or suite over 250. Total initial investment for a newly constructed 176-suite hotel ranges from $59,106,260 to $90,910,623, excluding real property. The monthly royalty ramps from 3.5% of Gross Rooms Revenue in year 1 to 4.5% in year 2 and 5.5% for the remainder of the term (5.5% for change of ownership or re-licensing). Item 19 discloses 2025 performance for comparable hotels: average room rate $183.55, average occupancy 74.0%, average RevPAR $135.78, average RevPAR Index 120.9, plus Hilton Honors contribution tables. The brand ended 2025 with 239 hotels — all franchised, 0 company-owned.
