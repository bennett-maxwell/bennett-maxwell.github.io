Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.36 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, the franchisor grants an exclusive territory: while your agreement runs, the franchisor may not place another franchisee — or operate a competing company unit — inside your territory's boundaries (subject to the carve-outs in the FDD). Areas inside an existing franchisee's territory are therefore not available to a new buyer.

What is NOT available — and where the "most likely available" label applies

Not available: any area inside an existing franchisee's exclusive territory while that agreement runs. Existing units sit in: AL, AR, CA, CO, CT, DE, FL, GA, ID, IL, IN, IA, KY, LA, MD, MA, MI, MN, MS, MO, NE, NJ, NY, OH, OK, OR, PA, RI, TN, TX, UT, VA, WA, WI — a specific address near an existing unit is governed by that territory's boundaries.

Most likely available — no existing or announced outlets here, based on the brand's latest FDD (Item 20): the FDD discloses no existing units and no projected openings in AK, DC, HI, KS, ME, MT, NV, NH, NM, NC, ND, SC, SD, VT, WV, WY. Only the franchisor can confirm availability.
