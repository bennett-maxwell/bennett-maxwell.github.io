About Jersey Mike's Subs

JERSEY MIKE'S SUBS is America's fastest-growing sub sandwich franchise with 3,227 locations systemwide (3,201 franchised + 26 company-owned) as of 12/28/2025 (Item 20, Table No. 1, PDF p.53). The initial franchise fee is $20,000 per restaurant (Item 5, PDF p.9); ongoing royalty is 6.5% of gross receipts (Item 6, PDF p.10). Total estimated initial investment ranges $436,176 to $1,162,228 (Item 7, PDF pp.17-18). Ownership is owner-operator: franchisees must devote full-time energy and best efforts to restaurant operations (Item 15, PDF p.42). [FDD: A Sub Above, LLC, 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document, issued April 10, 2026] TERRITORY (Item 12): territory is NOT exclusive - the FDD states you will not receive an exclusive territory.

Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Protected territory — classified from Item 12, p.42 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, the territory carries some protection but not full exclusivity — for example, the franchisor may reserve certain channels, accounts, or future concepts. Areas inside an existing franchisee's protected territory are generally not available to a new buyer for a directly competing unit; the exact limits are in the FDD text quoted above.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “territory is NOT exclusive - the FDD states you will not receive an exclusive territory.”
