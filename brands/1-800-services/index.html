Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.36 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, the franchisor grants an exclusive territory: while your agreement runs, the franchisor may not place another franchisee — or operate a competing company unit — inside your territory's boundaries (subject to the carve-outs in the FDD). Areas inside an existing franchisee's territory are therefore not available to a new buyer.

How to read this catalog. Every figure comes from the brand's own Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) — franchise fees and investment ranges from Items 5–7, royalty from Item 6, territory rules from Item 12, and outlet counts by state from Item 20. Territory availability is shown the way the FDD supports it: we state what a brand's territory rule says and where its existing units are.
