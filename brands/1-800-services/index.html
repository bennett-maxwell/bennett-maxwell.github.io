Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.36 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, the franchisor grants an exclusive territory: while your agreement runs, the franchisor may not place another franchisee — or operate a competing company unit — inside your territory's boundaries (subject to the carve-outs in the FDD).
