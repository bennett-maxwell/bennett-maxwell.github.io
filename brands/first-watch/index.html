About First Watch

First Watch is a daytime-only dining concept serving made-from-scratch breakfast, brunch, and lunch. Known for seasonal menus and elevated day-dining, First Watch operates 400+ restaurants and never opens for dinner. Founded in 1983 in Pacific Grove, California, the company is headquartered in Bradenton, Florida and trades on NASDAQ as FWRG.
