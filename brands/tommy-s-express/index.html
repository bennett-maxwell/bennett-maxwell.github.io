About TOMMY'S EXPRESS

We offer franchises for the operation of Tommy's Express Car Wash businesses ("Tommy's Business"). We license a generic version of our monthly membership platform, as well as our license plate recognition software, to non-franchise car wash operators for a fee. Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Protected territory — classified from Item 12, p.49 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, the territory carries some protection but not full exclusivity — for example, the franchisor may reserve certain channels, accounts, or future concepts. Areas inside an existing franchisee's protected territory are generally not available to a new buyer for a directly competing unit; the exact limits are in the FDD text quoted above.
