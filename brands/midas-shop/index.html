About MIDAS SHOP

Midas is one of the world's largest providers of automotive service, with over 2,200 franchised and company-owned locations. Founded in 1956 and headquartered in Palm Beach Gardens, FL, Midas offers complete car care including brakes, tires, exhaust, and oil changes.
