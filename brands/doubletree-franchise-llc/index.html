About Doubletree Franchise LLC

DoubleTree by Hilton is a hotels & resorts franchise brand offered by HILTON FRANCHISE HOLDING LLC (2026 US FDD, issued March 30, 2026). The franchise application fee for new development or conversion is $85,000, plus $400 for each additional guest room or suite over 250 ($175,000 for change of ownership; $85,000 for re-licensing). Total initial investment for a newly constructed 225-room DoubleTree hotel ranges from $31,454,554 to $108,661,784, excluding real property (DoubleTree Suites 225-room: $33,007,054 to $126,260,147). The monthly royalty fee is a flat 5% of Gross Rooms Revenue, payable monthly by the 15th day of the following month. Item 19 discloses 2025 performance for 294 comparable hotels (24 company-managed, 270 franchisee-managed): average room rate $159.90, average occupancy 68.4%, average RevPAR $109.35, average RevPAR Index 105.2, Hilton Honors contribution to occupancy 68.5% and to room nights 39,569 average. The brand ended 2025 with 372 hotels — all franchised, 0 company-owned.
