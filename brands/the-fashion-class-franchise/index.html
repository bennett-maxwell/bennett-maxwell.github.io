About The Fashion Class Franchise

We only offer franchises which operate under the “The Fashion Class” Marks. The Franchise Offered: We offer franchises for the right to operate a sewing and design business which offers on-site and off-site classes, camps, birthday parties, and special events business under the “The Fashion Class”… TERRITORY (Item 12): this franchisor grants an exclusive territory as described in its FDD.

Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.21 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, the franchisor grants an exclusive territory: while your agreement runs, the franchisor may not place another franchisee — or operate a competing company unit — inside your territory's boundaries (subject to the carve-outs in the FDD). Areas inside an existing franchisee's territory are therefore not available to a new buyer.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “this franchisor grants an exclusive territory as described in its FDD.”

How to read this catalog.
