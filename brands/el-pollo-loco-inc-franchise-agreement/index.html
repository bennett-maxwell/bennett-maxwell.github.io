About EL POLLO LOCO INC FRANCHISE AGREEMENT

El Pollo Loco is a Mexican-style quick-service restaurant chain famous for its flame-grilled chicken marinated in citrus and herbs. Founded in 1975 in Mexico and introduced to the US market in 1980, El Pollo Loco operates approximately 500 locations primarily in California and the Southwest. Franchisees benefit from a distinctive menu differentiator, strong brand heritage, and a loyal consumer base seeking flavorful higher-quality fast-casual Mexican cuisine.
