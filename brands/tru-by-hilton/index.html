Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.63 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, the franchisor grants an exclusive territory: while your agreement runs, the franchisor may not place another franchisee — or operate a competing company unit — inside your territory's boundaries (subject to the carve-outs in the FDD). Areas inside an existing franchisee's territory are therefore not available to a new buyer.

What is NOT available — and where the "most likely available" label applies

Not available: any area inside an existing franchisee's exclusive territory while that agreement runs. Existing units sit in: AL, AZ, AR, CA, CO, DE, FL, GA, ID, IL, IN, IA, KS, KY, LA, ME, MD, MA, MI, MN, MS, MO, NE, NV, NH, NM, NY, NC, OH, OK, OR, PA, SC, SD, TN, TX, UT, VA, WA, WV, WI, WY — a specific address near an existing unit is governed by that territory's boundaries.

Most likely available — no existing or announced outlets here, based on the brand's latest FDD (Item 20): the FDD discloses no existing units and no projected openings in AK, CT, DC, HI, MT, NJ, ND, RI, VT. Only the franchisor can confirm availability.
