About FANTASTIC SAMS

Fantastic Sams is a full-service hair salon franchise founded in 1974 in Memphis, Tennessee. As one of the oldest and largest hair salon chains in North America, Fantastic Sams offers haircuts, color, styling, and other hair care services for men, women, and children. With approximately 1,100 locations, the brand is known for providing professional salon services at affordable prices in convenient neighborhood locations.
