About Firehouse Subs

FIREHOUSE SUBS is a fast-casual restaurant franchise with 1,200+ locations serving premium hot submarine sandwiches inspired by the fire service tradition. The brand's commitment to the Firehouse Subs Public Safety Foundation differentiates it with a cause-marketing story that resonates with communities, offering franchisees a differentiated QSR concept with strong brand loyalty and multi-unit development opportunities.
