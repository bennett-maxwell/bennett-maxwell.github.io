About The UPS Store, Inc

The UPS Store is the largest U.S. franchisor of retail shipping, postal, print and business service centers, with 5,487 franchised locations and 16 company-owned at end-2025 (2026 FDD, Item 20, printed p.80). The initial franchise fee is $39,950 for a first Center (Item 5, printed p.15). Ongoing royalty is 5% of STR plus a 1% marketing fund and 2.5% national advertising (Item 6, printed p.23). Total estimated initial investment ranges $222,368 to $606,081 (traditional Item 7, attested via 2026-FDD extracts — sharpsheets itemized extract and vetmyfranchise 2026 snapshot; the 2026 Traditional FDD PDF is not publicly released). Fee, royalty and unit figures are byte-verified from the 2026 Non-Traditional FDD. [FDD: The UPS Store, Inc., 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document, issued April 23, 2026] Ownership: the owner need not be the on-premises operator, but a full-time on-premises Primary Operator (owner or supervisory employee) is required, plus training (Item 15, printed p.70, 2026 FDD issued April 23, 2026).
