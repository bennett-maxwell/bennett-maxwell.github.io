About World of Sourdough Franchising, LLC

World of Sourdough Franchising, LLC (d/b/a World of Sourdough) is a Texas LLC founded in 2021 offering handcrafted-sandwich restaurant franchises under the WOSD marks from its Granite Bay, California headquarters. The FDD issued February 18, 2025 sets the initial franchise fee at $49,500 and the estimated initial investment between $285,800 and $568,725, including a $150,000 Area Development Fee. Franchisees pay a 5% royalty on gross revenues, due weekly every Sunday. Each franchisee receives a protected territory of a 2-mile radius around its approved specific location, excluding indoor malls, airports, college campuses, military bases, and in-store retail sites. Item 20 reports 80 total outlets at end of 2024 — 78 franchised across Arizona, California, Colorado, Nevada, Oregon, and Texas, plus 2 company/affiliate-owned — with zero terminations, non-renewals, or ceased operations in 2022-2024. A settlement with West Coast Sourdough, Inc. bars any WOSD restaurant within 3 miles of an existing West Coast Sourdough store.
