About Liberty Tax Service

TERRITORY (Item 12): protected — The territory is non-exclusive but protected from other Liberty-branded physical locations; it typically covers an area generating 7,000 to 8,500 paid federal tax returns, with restrictions on franchisor competition within the defined geographic area. OUTLETS (Item 20): 1663 total (1537 franchised + 126 company-owned). ITEMS 1-23: Fee: The standard price for one new Liberty Tax territory is a $25,000 initial franchise fee, of which $10,000 is paid as a deposit after you have held Royalty: After you open, the two ongoing brand charges are a 14% royalty on Gross Receipts and a separate 5% advertising fee on the same receipts. Investment: For one new franchise agreement, Liberty estimates you need about $49,700 to $71,400 before royalties, advertising fees, and interest. Training: Before you open, you and any general manager must finish a minimum 3-day classroom Initial Owners Training plus 12-15 hours online, and a one-day Territory: You do not get an exclusive territory and can face competition from other franchisees, company outlets, and other channels. Operations: You do not have to own and run the office yourself, but someone must. Term: The franchise term is 5 years and can renew for another 5 years if you are not in default, with no renewal fee and no increase in royalties or Financials: Liberty does give a financial performance representation for prep fees and federal return counts, not a full profit picture. Legal: Liberty is an active plaintiff against former franchisees for contract, non-compete, trademark, and debt claims, and it also discloses older cases Outlets: The franchised office count has fallen every year in this disclosure, from 1,991 at the start of 2023 to 1,537 at the end of 2025, while Liberty Tax Service franchises operate tax return preparation offices using special marketing techniques and operating procedures.
