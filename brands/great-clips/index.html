About Great Clips

GREAT CLIPS is the world's largest hair salon franchise with 4,400+ salons across North America. The brand offers franchisees a scalable, simple-to-operate business model with no appointment needed, a proven clip-and-trim menu, and robust technology including online check-in that drives traffic and simplifies staffing for multi-unit operators.
