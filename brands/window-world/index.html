About Window World

Window World is America's largest replacement window and exterior remodeling franchise, offering windows, doors, siding, and roofing. Founded in 1995, Window World operates over 200 franchise locations and has installed over 20 million windows. TERRITORY (Item 12): this franchisor grants an exclusive territory as described in its FDD.

Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.52 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, the franchisor grants an exclusive territory: while your agreement runs, the franchisor may not place another franchisee — or operate a competing company unit — inside your territory's boundaries (subject to the carve-outs in the FDD). Areas inside an existing franchisee's territory are therefore not available to a new buyer.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “this franchisor grants an exclusive territory as described in its FDD.”

What is NOT available — and where availability is most plausible

Not available: any area inside an existing franchisee's exclusive territory while that agreement runs. Existing units sit in: AL, AZ, AR, CA, CO, CT, DE, FL, GA, HI, ID, IL, IN, IA, KS, KY, LA, ME, MD, MA, MI, MN, MS, MO, NE, NV, NJ, NM, NY, ND, OH, OK, PA, RI, SD, TN, TX, UT, VT, VA, WA, WV, WI — a specific address near an existing unit is governed by that territory's boundaries.

Most plausible availability (inference, not a promise): the FDD discloses no existing units and no projected openings in AK, DC, NH, NC, SC, WY. Combined with the Item 12 rule above, these are the areas where availability is most plausible. This is an inference from the absence of disclosed units — only the franchisor can confirm a specific territory.
