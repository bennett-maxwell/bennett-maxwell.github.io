What is NOT available — and where availability is most plausible

Not available: any area inside an existing franchisee's protected territory while that agreement runs. Existing units sit in: AZ, CA, FL, KS, LA, MD, MI, MO, NV, NC, OH, SC, TX, VA, WA, WI — a specific address near an existing unit is governed by that territory's boundaries.

Most plausible availability (inference, not a promise): the FDD discloses no existing units and no projected openings in AL, AK, AR, CO, CT, DE, DC, GA, HI, ID, IL, IN, IA, KY, ME, MA, MN, MS, MT, NE, NH, NJ, NM, NY, ND, OK, OR, PA, RI, SD, TN, UT, VT, WV, WY. Combined with the Item 12 rule above, these are the areas where availability is most plausible. This is an inference from the absence of disclosed units — only the franchisor can confirm a specific territory.
