About Dunkin'

MONEY FIGURES (Dunkin' Donuts Franchising LLC 2026 FDD, issued Mar 26, 2026): initial franchise fee $40,000-$90,000 depending on Development Area Type (Item 5, PDF p32 L45); estimated initial investment for a freestanding restaurant $532,400-$1,832,500 (Item 7 Table A, PDF p53 L13-14); shopping center/storefront, gas station/convenience and non-traditional (SDO) formats carry lower ranges (cover, PDF p1 L14-22); continuing franchise fee (royalty) 5.9% of gross sales, advertising 5.0% (2.5% for SDOs) (Item 6, PDF p44 L13); 9,999 Dunkin' restaurants operating in the US at fiscal 2025 year-end (9,963 franchised + 36 company-owned), of which 8,744 single-branded (Item 1, PDF p9 L17-18); Item 20 outlet summary shows 8,780 single-brand US outlets at 2025 year-end (Item 20 Table 1, PDF p99 L36).
