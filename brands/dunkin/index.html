About Dunkin'

MONEY FIGURES (Dunkin' Donuts Franchising LLC 2026 FDD, issued Mar 26, 2026): initial franchise fee $40,000-$90,000 depending on Development Area Type (Item 5, PDF p32 L45); estimated initial investment for a freestanding restaurant $532,400-$1,832,500 (Item 7 Table A, PDF p53 L13-14); shopping center/storefront, gas station/convenience and non-traditional (SDO) formats carry lower ranges (cover, PDF p1 L14-22); continuing franchise fee (royalty) 5.9% of gross sales, advertising 5.0% (2.5% for SDOs) (Item 6, PDF p44 L13); 9,999 Dunkin' restaurants operating in the US at fiscal 2025 year-end (9,963 franchised + 36 company-owned), of which 8,744 single-branded (Item 1, PDF p9 L17-18); Item 20 outlet summary shows 8,780 single-brand US outlets at 2025 year-end (Item 20 Table 1, PDF p99 L36).

Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Non-exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.83 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, you do not receive an exclusive territory. The franchisor may sell additional franchises, operate company units, or sell through other channels in the same area. No area is reserved for you — and no area is closed to you by another franchisee's exclusivity either; placement is the franchisor's decision.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “territory is NOT exclusive - the FDD states you will not receive an exclusive territory. Availability

Because this brand grants no exclusive territory, there are no territory-based exclusions: no area is reserved for an existing franchisee, and no area is reserved for you. Whether a new unit is placed anywhere is the franchisor's decision under Item 12.
