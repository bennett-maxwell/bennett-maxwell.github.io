About The Hot Spot

The Hot Spot is a registered franchise offering services in the Other sector. TERRITORY (Item 12): this franchisor grants an exclusive territory as described in its FDD.

Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.25 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, the franchisor grants an exclusive territory: while your agreement runs, the franchisor may not place another franchisee — or operate a competing company unit — inside your territory's boundaries (subject to the carve-outs in the FDD). Areas inside an existing franchisee's territory are therefore not available to a new buyer.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “this franchisor grants an exclusive territory as described in its FDD.”

What is NOT available — and where the "most likely available" label applies

Not available: any area inside an existing franchisee's exclusive territory while that agreement runs. Existing units sit in: CA, FL, GA, HI, ID, NJ, NY, NC, OR, PA, TX — a specific address near an existing unit is governed by that territory's boundaries.
