About Salad House

Salad House is a fast-casual restaurant franchise developed by Salad House Franchising, LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company established October 5, 2016 and headquartered at 133 Prospect Street, Suite 1, Westfield, New Jersey 07092. The franchised restaurant serves a menu of fresh salads, soups, wraps, sandwiches, grain bowls, other food items prepared to order, and beverages for on-premises dining, carryout, catering, and delivery. Each restaurant operates from a single approved location within a designated territory that is generally a three-mile radius in rural areas or a seven-block radius in urban areas, and no exclusive territory is granted. The initial franchise fee is $40,000, the royalty is a continuing monthly fee of 6% of gross sales, and multi-unit development is available for 2 to 10 restaurants at a development area fee of $30,000 per additional restaurant. As of December 31, 2024 the system comprised 20 total outlets - 18 franchised and 2 company-owned - across New Jersey and New York, with zero transfers, terminations, or non-renewals in 2022-2024.
