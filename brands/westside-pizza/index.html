Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Non-exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.28 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, you do not receive an exclusive territory. The franchisor may sell additional franchises, operate company units, or sell through other channels in the same area. No area is reserved for you — and no area is closed to you by another franchisee's exclusivity either; placement is the franchisor's decision.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “territory is NOT exclusive - the FDD states you will not receive an exclusive territory. The grant of a franchise authorizes you to engage in our complete system under the name Westside Pizza® and other proprietary marks. You may also encounter competition from other Westside”
