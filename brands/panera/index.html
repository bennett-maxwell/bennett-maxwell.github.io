Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Non-exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.42 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, you do not receive an exclusive territory. The franchisor may sell additional franchises, operate company units, or sell through other channels in the same area. No area is reserved for you — and no area is closed to you by another franchisee's exclusivity either; placement is the franchisor's decision.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “territory is NOT exclusive - the FDD states you will not receive an exclusive territory. Unless the context otherwise requires, all references to “Panera,” “we,” “us,” or “our” refer to Panera, LLC and all references to “Franchisee,” “you,” or “your” refer to the person who is”

Availability

Because this brand grants no exclusive territory, there are no territory-based exclusions: no area is reserved for an existing franchisee, and no area is reserved for you. Whether a new unit is placed anywhere is the franchisor's decision under Item 12.

How to read this catalog. Every figure comes from the brand's own Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) — franchise fees and investment ranges from Items 5–7, royalty from Item 6, territory rules from Item 12, and outlet counts by state from Item 20. Territory availability is shown the way the FDD supports it: we state what a brand's territory rule says and where its existing units are. We never claim a specific area is available — only the franchisor can confirm that. Data assembled 2026-10-26; verify current terms with the franchisor before investing.
