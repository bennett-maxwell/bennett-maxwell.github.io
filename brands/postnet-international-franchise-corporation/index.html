About PostNet International Franchise Corporation

The initial investment required to open a PostNet center starts at approximately $223,000. Why are PostNet’s startup costs low? Because unlike other printing franchises, PostNet doesn’t require substantial investments in printing technology or machinery. It costs on average between $230,000 – $297,000 to start a PostNet franchised store. The total investment necessary to begin operating a PostNet franchise ranges from $223,207 to $289,807.
