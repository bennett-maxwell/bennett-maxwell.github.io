About NATHAN'S FAMOUSNATHAN'S

Nathan's Famous is an iconic American restaurant franchise celebrated for its world-famous hot dogs, crinkle-cut fries, and classic boardwalk fare. Founded in 1916 at Coney Island, New York, Nathan's Famous has become a beloved American institution with approximately 250 locations. The brand is synonymous with the July 4th Hot Dog Eating Contest. Franchisees gain access to a highly recognized heritage brand with diverse location formats including traditional restaurants, food courts, and non-traditional venues.
