About @SecFranOpBOrg

When franchisors set their franchise fee, which is essentially a licensing fee, they typically choose a price that is both attractive to prospective franchisees and comparable to competitors in their market. The fee also may depend on how much the franchisor expects it will need to cover the cost of salespeople and any initial resources provided to new franchises. How to read this catalog. Every figure comes from the brand's own Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) — franchise fees and investment ranges from Items 5–7, royalty from Item 6, territory rules from Item 12, and outlet counts by state from Item 20. Territory availability is shown the way the FDD supports it: we state what a brand's territory rule says and where its existing units are. We never claim a specific area is available — only the franchisor can confirm that. Data assembled 2026-10-26; verify current terms with the franchisor before investing.
