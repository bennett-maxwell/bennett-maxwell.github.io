Every Homewood Suites hotel provides guests with all the comforts and conveniences of home at a reasonable price. MONEY FIGURES (Homewood Suites 2026 US FDD, issued March 30, 2026): franchise application fee $100,000 for new development or conversion, plus $400 for each guest room or suite over 150 ($200,000 for change of ownership; $100,000 for re-licensing) (Item 5, doc p15); total investment $23,757,245–$34,728,465 for a newly constructed 131-suite hotel, excluding real property (up to $261,924 payable to the franchisor or affiliates) (Item 7, doc p36); monthly royalty 3.5% of Gross Rooms Revenue in year 1, 4.5% in year 2, 5.5% for the remainder of the term — 5.5% for change of ownership or re-licensing (Item 6, doc p20); Item 19 FPR PRESENT — 2025 comparable-hotel tables for room rate, occupancy, RevPAR, RevPAR Index, and Hilton Honors contribution to occupancy and room nights (doc pp78–84); 531 hotels at year-end 2025 — all franchised, 0 company-owned (Item 20 Table 1, doc p85).
