About BUFFALO WILD WINGS GO

Buffalo Wild Wings GO is a fast-casual spinoff of the iconic Buffalo Wild Wings brand, featuring wings, beer, and sports in a streamlined smaller-format restaurant. With the parent brand founded in 1982, Buffalo Wild Wings GO offers franchisees access to a beloved brand with passionate sports fans and wing enthusiasts. The GO format requires lower investment than traditional locations while delivering the same bold flavors and game-day experience customers crave.
