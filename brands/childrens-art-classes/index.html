Territory description (Item 12): The franchise agreement grants an exclusive territory of about 45,000 to 55,000 people, at least 15% children under 18, or a three-mile radius if not stated in writing within 90 days after opening. The franchisor will not open a company-owned or franchised Children's Art Classes outlet in that territory. A multi-unit development agreement does not grant an exclusive area-developer territory. (Item 12, p.32-34 of the FDD)

Training: Franchisees must complete the “pre-opening” portion of initial training program between 30 and 45 days prior to opening the business, and the franchisees must complete the “post training” portion prior to opening. (Item 11, p.26 of the FDD)

Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.32 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, the franchisor grants an exclusive territory: while your agreement runs, the franchisor may not place another franchisee — or operate a competing company unit — inside your territory's boundaries (subject to the carve-outs in the FDD). Areas inside an existing franchisee's territory are therefore not available to a new buyer.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “this franchisor grants an exclusive territory as described in its FDD.”
