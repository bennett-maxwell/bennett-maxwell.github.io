About Popeyes Louisiana Kitchen

POPEYES LOUISIANA KITCHEN is one of the world's largest chicken quick-service restaurant brands with 3,700+ locations globally. Owned by Restaurant Brands International, it offers franchisees proven brand strength, a simplified menu anchored by the iconic chicken sandwich, strong AUVs, and development incentives for multi-unit operators.
