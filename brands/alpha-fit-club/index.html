About Alpha Fit Club

Alpha Fit Club studios offer circuit-style group fitness training focused on strength training and HIIT-style cardio for adults of all ages. Each studio operates from an approved retail location within a designated territory, typically a three-mile radius. Franchisees pay an 8% royalty on gross sales plus a 1% brand development fund fee.
