Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Protected territory — classified from Item 12, p.48 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, the territory carries some protection but not full exclusivity — for example, the franchisor may reserve certain channels, accounts, or future concepts. Areas inside an existing franchisee's protected territory are generally not available to a new buyer for a directly competing unit; the exact limits are in the FDD text quoted above.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “territory is NOT exclusive - the FDD states you will not receive an exclusive territory. You will provide products to customers under the “Home Frite” Marks, using our distinctive operating procedures and standards in a limited protected territory and from a single location”

How to read this catalog. Every figure comes from the brand's own Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) — franchise fees and investment ranges from Items 5–7, royalty from Item 6, territory rules from Item 12, and outlet counts by state from Item 20. Territory availability is shown the way the FDD supports it: we state what a brand's territory rule says and where its existing units are. We never claim a specific area is available — only the franchisor can confirm that. Data assembled 2026-10-26; verify current terms with the franchisor before investing.
