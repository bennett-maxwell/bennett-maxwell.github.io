About TACO BELL TRADITIONAL (UNIT)

Taco Bell is one of America's most popular quick-service restaurant chains serving Mexican-inspired food including tacos, burritos, and quesadillas. Founded in 1962 in Irvine, California, Taco Bell operates over 8,200 locations worldwide. Franchisees benefit from one of the most recognized fast-food brands globally, with strong marketing support, innovative menu development, and a dedicated customer base that drives consistent traffic and sales volume.
