About SUPERCUTS

MONEY FIGURES (Supercuts, Inc. FDD, issued Oct 17, 2025, as amended Feb 1, 2026 — 2025, latest public (no pure-2026 issuance publicly available)): initial development fee $39,500 for a single salon; Supercuts charges no separate initial franchise fee under the Development Agreement (Item 5, PDF p23 L11; p22 L33); estimated initial investment $185,930-$323,460 including initial lease costs (Item 7, PDF p32 L30-31); royalty 4% of combined net service and merchandise revenue from opening through the first-year anniversary, then 6% through the end of the term (Item 6, PDF p25 L39-55); 1,801 total outlets at fiscal 2025 year-end (1,701 franchised + 100 company-owned) (Item 20 Table 1, PDF p68 L18).
