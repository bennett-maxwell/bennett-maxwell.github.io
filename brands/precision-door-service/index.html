About PRECISION DOOR SERVICE

Precision Door Service is the largest residential garage door service franchise in the United States, founded in 1999 and headquartered in Tempe, AZ. With approximately 100 franchise locations nationwide, Precision Door specializes in garage door repair, replacement, and installation services. The brand is known for its 24/7 service availability, same-day appointments, and lifetime warranties, making it the preferred choice for homeowners.
