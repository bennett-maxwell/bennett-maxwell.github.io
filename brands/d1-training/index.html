About D1 Training

D1 Training is an athletic-based training franchise founded in 2001 and franchising since 2017, headquartered in Franklin, Tennessee. Its facilities offer scholastic and adult group training, coaching, and personal training under the D1 name. The 2025 FDD discloses a $59,500 franchise fee, a 7% royalty, a 2% brand fund contribution, and an estimated initial investment of $480,557 to $933,432. Franchisees receive a non-exclusive Designated Territory sized as a 0.20-mile to 3-mile radius around the approved facility, based on qualifying households in the area. The system reported 129 outlets at the end of 2024 (127 franchised, 2 company-owned) with 54 projected new franchised openings.
