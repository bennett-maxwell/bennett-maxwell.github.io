About Potbelly Sandwich Shop

Potbelly Sandwich Shop is a fast-casual restaurant chain known for its warm toasted sandwiches, soups, salads, and live music atmosphere. Founded in 1977 in Chicago as an antique store, Potbelly evolved into a beloved sandwich brand with approximately 430 locations across the United States. Franchisees benefit from a distinctive brand identity, loyal customer following, and a menu centered on fresh ingredients and high-quality toasted sandwiches made to order.

Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Non-exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.51 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, you do not receive an exclusive territory. The franchisor may sell additional franchises, operate company units, or sell through other channels in the same area. No area is reserved for you — and no area is closed to you by another franchisee's exclusivity either; placement is the franchisor's decision.
