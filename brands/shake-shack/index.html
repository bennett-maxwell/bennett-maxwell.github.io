About Shake Shack

Shake Shack is a premium fast-casual burger stand offering all-natural Angus beef burgers, flat-top dogs, crinkle-cut fries, and hand-spun frozen custard shakes. Born in NYC, Shake Shack now operates globally with 500+ locations. Founded by Danny Meyer, Shake Shack began as a hot dog cart in Madison Square Park in 2001 before opening its first permanent kiosk in 2004.
