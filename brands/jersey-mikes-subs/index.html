About JERSEY MIKE'S SUBS

Jersey Mike's Subs is a fast-casual sub sandwich franchise founded in 1956 in Manasquan, New Jersey. The brand is known for its fresh-sliced, fresh-grilled subs made from high-quality ingredients. With over 2,600 locations across the United States, Jersey Mike's is one of the fastest-growing sub sandwich chains, celebrated for its authentic Jersey Shore taste and community commitment.
