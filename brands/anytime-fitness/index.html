About Anytime Fitness

MONEY FIGURES (Anytime Fitness FDD 2026, issued Mar 31, 2026): standard initial franchise fee $42,500 (Item 5, PDF p15); estimated initial investment $539,329-$905,482 (Item 7, PDF p26; cover PDF p1); Monthly Fee $842/mo per center, CPI-adjusted each January; franchisor may replace it with a percentage-based monthly royalty of up to 8% of Gross Revenue on 30 days notice (Item 6, PDF p18, p23); General Advertising and Marketing Fee $900/mo per center (Item 6, PDF p18). NOTE: the Royalty tile percent is not FDD-grounded - the FDD charges a flat $842/mo Monthly Fee, not a percentage royalty (schema cannot represent a flat fee; left as-is). OUTLETS (FDD Item 20 Table 1, PDF p68): 2,282 total outlets at end of 2025 (2,301 at start; net change -19). OWNERSHIP (FDD Item 15, PDF p56): franchisee not required to personally supervise; if not the on-premises supervisor, must designate a Principal Operator as on-premises supervisor. TERRITORY (FDD Item 12, PDF p52): protected (non-exclusive) circle, radius no larger than 3 miles, population no more than 30,000.
