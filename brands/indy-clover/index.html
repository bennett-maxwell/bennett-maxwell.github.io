About Indy Clover

Indy Clover is a high-end women's clothing thrift and consignment franchise. Two revenue streams in one store: (1) booth rentals for individual sellers (~60 booths at $30/week = ~$7K/month before sales), and (2) consignment buy-in stock the franchisee owns and resells at markup. 1,800-3,000 sq ft retail concept. Headquartered in Lindon, Utah. OUTLETS (FDD Item 20, Table 1 — 2026 FDD, PDF p.52): 17 total outlets as of Jun 30, 2025 (14 franchised + 3 company-owned). 150,000+ sellers, 3M+ items sold, $15M+ paid to sellers system-wide. Ownership (FDD Item 15, 2026 FDD, PDF p.41): the owner must participate fully in the day-to-day operation of the franchise business; with franchisor approval, a manager may be designated after 3 months of operations. NON-exclusive territory (5 driving-mile radius typical, defined per FDD Item 12). Most US territories still open. Money figures from the 2026 FDD (issued Feb 4, 2026): total investment $174,450–$225,800 (Item 7, p.20); initial franchise fee $35,000 (Item 5, p.12); royalty 5% of monthly Post-Seller Profit (Item 6, p.13); National Marketing Fee $1,200/month (Item 6, pp.13-14).

Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Non-exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.35 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, you do not receive an exclusive territory. The franchisor may sell additional franchises, operate company units, or sell through other channels in the same area. No area is reserved for you — and no area is closed to you by another franchisee's exclusivity either; placement is the franchisor's decision.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “territory is NOT exclusive - the FDD states you will not receive an exclusive territory.”

Availability

Because this brand grants no exclusive territory, there are no territory-based exclusions: no area is reserved for an existing franchisee, and no area is reserved for you. Whether a new unit is placed anywhere is the franchisor's decision under Item 12.
