About PET SUPPLIES PLUS

Pet Supplies Plus is one of the largest pet retail franchises in the U.S. with over 700 locations. Founded in 1988 and headquartered in Livonia, MI, Pet Supplies Plus offers a neighborhood pet store experience with a wide range of pet food, supplies, and services. Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.43 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, the franchisor grants an exclusive territory: while your agreement runs, the franchisor may not place another franchisee — or operate a competing company unit — inside your territory's boundaries (subject to the carve-outs in the FDD). Areas inside an existing franchisee's territory are therefore not available to a new buyer.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “this franchisor grants an exclusive territory as described in its FDD.”
