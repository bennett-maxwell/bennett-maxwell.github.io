About Ace Handyman Franchising, Inc

Ace Handyman Services, formerly known as Handyman Matters, is a trusted home repair franchise headquartered in Denver, CO. Founded in 1998 and now backed by Ace Hardware's powerful brand recognition, the franchise offers comprehensive handyman services including carpentry, plumbing, electrical, and general home maintenance. Territory — what the FDD says (Item 12)

Non-exclusive territory — classified from Item 12, p.38 of the brand's FDD

Under this brand's Item 12 rule, you do not receive an exclusive territory. The franchisor may sell additional franchises, operate company units, or sell through other channels in the same area. No area is reserved for you — and no area is closed to you by another franchisee's exclusivity either; placement is the franchisor's decision.

The FDD's own words (Item 12): “territory is NOT exclusive - the FDD states you will not receive an exclusive territory. We are a franchising company that sells and grants franchises for the operation of businesses (an “AHS Business”) using the name “ACE HANDYMAN SERVICES.” We have been offering franchised”

Availability

Because this brand grants no exclusive territory, there are no territory-based exclusions: no area is reserved for an existing franchisee, and no area is reserved for you. Whether a new unit is placed anywhere is the franchisor's decision under Item 12.
