Work with a vetted consultant

Buying a franchise is a six-figure decision made from documents most buyers read once. A consultant's job is to help you read them like an owner: the FDD, the Item 19 numbers, the Item 12 territory rule, and the questions the franchisor would rather you didn't ask. When you're ready to talk to franchisors, this is how the FranchiseKI consultant network works.

Vetted, Not Random

Consultants in the network are vetted — you're matched with a consultant for your goals and territory, not handed a random name. The match starts from your own research: your PerfectFit results, the brands on your comparison list, and the territory you're targeting.

Free — Here's Why

Working with a consultant costs you nothing: consultants are paid by franchisors, not by buyers. The franchise fee you pay is the same whether or not a consultant is involved — the franchisor pays the consultant out of its franchise sales budget when a placement closes.

On Your Side of the Table

A consultant works on your side of the table — helping you evaluate brands, ask the hard questions, and move at your pace. That includes walking away: a good consultant will tell you when a brand's FDD doesn't support the pitch, and the due diligence dossiers on this site give you the sourced facts to check anyone's claims against, consultant included.

How it works

1

Take the PerfectFit Quiz, build a comparison, and read the dossier on each brand's page. You'll arrive with better questions.

Call customer service at (385) 475-5319 or email info@franchiseki.com with the brands and the state you're targeting, and you'll be matched with a vetted consultant.

How to read this catalog. Every figure comes from the brand's own Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) — franchise fees and investment ranges from Items 5–7, royalty from Item 6, territory rules from Item 12, and outlet counts by state from Item 20. Territory availability is shown the way the FDD supports it: we state what a brand's territory rule says and where its existing units are. We never claim a specific area is available — only the franchisor can confirm that. Data assembled 2026-10-26; verify current terms with the franchisor before investing.
